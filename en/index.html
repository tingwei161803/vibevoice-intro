Open-source long-form voice AI

VibeVoice synthesises up to 90 minutes of natural, multi-speaker audio in a single pass — powered by a 7.5 Hz continuous speech tokenizer and a next-token diffusion design. Six open checkpoints now cover long-form TTS, real-time speech, long-form and streaming recognition, and CPU-only edge inference.
